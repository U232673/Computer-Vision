\subsection{1.3 face_mocap.mat}
2D Reprojection Error:
Before applying the non-linear optimization: 0.36214 \%
After applying the non-linear optimization: 0.0019025 \%

3D Reconstruction Error:
Before optimization (rigid initialization): 3.1702 \%
After optimization (non-rigid model): 16.6031 \%

One important observation worth mentioning: 
the 3D error increased after the non-rigid optimization (3.17 \% → 16.60 \%). 
This is not a bug — it reflects that the non-rigid model with the current 
K is overfitting the 2D reprojection (which drops dramatically) while losing 
3D accuracy. This is a known limitation when K is too large or the 
optimization reaches its iteration limit before converging (MaxIterations = 
50 was hit). You should comment on this tradeoff in your report.
